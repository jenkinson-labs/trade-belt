Trade Belt is made for the people who build, fix and fit.

Whether you work alone or run a small firm with subcontractors, the evenings look the same: quotes to write, photos to sort, messages to answer and invoices to chase. Trade Belt learns how you work, your prices, your wording and your trade, and takes that admin off your hands.

It's set up around each business, not one size fits all. A roofer's quote doesn't look like a plasterer's, and it shouldn't.

Trade Belt follows the job from the first enquiry to the final payment.

Most tools handle one piece of the job. Trade Belt keeps hold of the whole thing, so every message, photo and price stays attached to the job it belongs to, and nothing has to be typed up twice.

Quotes priced from your own list, not from a guess.

Describe the job and Trade Belt sets out the work by area, the way you'd write it yourself. Materials are priced from your price list, your labour rate and markup are added, and you get a branded PDF with a quote number, a valid-until date and a box for the client to sign.

If something's missing, like a price it can't find or a labour time you didn't mention, it tells you before anything is sent. The AI never makes up a number.

A record of the job, ready before anyone asks for it.

Every visit is dated and filed with its photos and videos. Changes the client asks for are noted as they happen. When a client questions what was agreed, the whole job comes out as one documentation pack.

On a bigger project, it's the site diary you never had time to keep. On a one-day job, it's proof of what you did and when. Either way, it's there before anyone asks for it.
